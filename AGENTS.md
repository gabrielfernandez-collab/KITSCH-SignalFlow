# KITSCH SignalFlow

## Product Purpose

KITSCH SignalFlow is a weekly competitive intelligence module designed to help KITSCH leadership understand:

* competitor launches
* pricing moves
* campaign angles
* emerging opportunities
* competitive risks

and determine what actions leadership should consider.

The product is intended as an Executive Intelligence Module rather than a traditional dashboard.

---

## Original Assessment Objective

Build a working module we can plug into our current dashboard — weekly competitive intel on hair accessories, consumables on-brand, and products in our landscape.

---

## Original Requirements

* Pulls only public data:

  * competitor sites
  * social
  * ad libraries

* Outputs a structured brief on:

  * competitor launches
  * pricing moves
  * campaign angles

* Demonstrates:

  * research depth
  * judgment calls
  * clear output structure

* Useful in week one

* Timebox:

  * 5 business days
  * something real
  * not over-engineered
  * clean and working
  * can be iterated on

---

## Evaluation Criteria

* How the candidate thinks through real problems
* Taste in what matters and what to ignore
* How output is structured so leadership can act on it
* Quality of build versus template polish
* Communication and check-ins

---

## Deliverables

* Working module
* Sample weekly brief

---

## Public Data Rules

Only public data sources.

No private APIs.

No credentials.

No restricted sources.

No unsupported scraping.

---

## MVP Collection Strategy

Website Sources

* Live public website collection when enabled
* Fallback extraction when blocked

Social Sources

* Public metadata/source connectors
* Expansion-ready architecture

Ad Library Sources

* Public reference connectors
* Expansion-ready architecture

Seeded Demonstration Signals

* Allowed when clearly labeled
* Never represented as unsupported live intelligence

---

## Intelligence Framework

Every surfaced signal should answer:

1. What happened?
2. Why it matters?
3. Recommended action
4. Evidence
5. Confidence
